FINDING 1/3  [Major] [contradiction]  (raised by consistency reviewer)
LOCATION: Part A §6.1 M9 row (L327) and §4.1 Public Verifier row (L180); Part C M9-14 (L1389 table) and M9 field table (L1464); Part C M8-61 (L1270) and M8-62
EXACT TEXT IN THE DOCUMENT:
| Status banner | Display | Yes | One of the five states | Icon plus words, never colour alone |
PROBLEM:
Four different status sets exist for one issued report. Section 1 says the public page shows "whether the document is genuine, its number and date, the issuing laboratory, a short sample description, the certificate's live status (valid, superseded, cancelled)" and its M9 one-liner says "confirms a document is genuine, current, superseded or cancelled" — i.e. three states including **Cancelled** and excluding Withdrawn. Part C M9-14 enumerates four banners: "**GENUINE — VALID**, **GENUINE — SUPERSEDED**, **GENUINE — WITHDRAWN**, or **NOT GENUINE**" — including Withdrawn and excluding Cancelled. The M9 field table then says "One of the five states" without naming five. M8-61 lists the register status as "(Issued / Superseded / Withdrawn)" while M8-62 flags any number "that is neither Issued nor Cancelled" as Missing, so Cancelled must also exist. Section 2 §10.1 confirms "A cancelled report keeps its number." The developer cannot enumerate the status column and the public page will show a status the register cannot produce.
REQUIRED FIX:
Make Part C authoritative and align the two outliers upward; do NOT invent a Cancelled report status or a fifth banner.

1. parts/section_1_foundation.md L98, L180 (§4.1 Public Verifier row) and L327 (§6.1 M9 row): replace "cancelled" with "withdrawn" — "genuine, current, superseded or withdrawn" and "the certificate's live status (valid, superseded, withdrawn)".

2. parts/section_5_nfr_plan.md L164 (NFR-69): replace "the words GENUINE / NOT GENUINE / SUPERSEDED / CANCELLED" with "the words GENUINE — VALID, GENUINE — SUPERSEDED, GENUINE — WITHDRAWN or NOT GENUINE, exactly as enumerated in M9-14".

3. parts/section_3_functional_core.md L1464 (M9 field table, Status banner row): replace "One of the five states" with an explicit cross-reference rather than a bare count — "One of the four banners of M9-14 (GENUINE — VALID / GENUINE — SUPERSEDED / GENUINE — WITHDRAWN / NOT GENUINE), plus the distinct signature-check wordings of M9-17, M9-18 and M9-19". This removes the ambiguity between the four banners and the five signature-check states without adding a status.

4. Add one glossary entry in parts/section_1_foundation.md beside "Superseded" (L296) locking the distinction, since it is the root cause: "Withdrawn — an issued report taken out of force entirely without replacement (M8-59); shown on the public verification page." and "Cancelled — a state of a NUMBER in a gap-free series whose document was never issued (WF-100, WF-102, M8-62). It is never a report lifecycle status and never appears on the public verification page, because only issued report versions receive a verification token (M9-09)."

5. Leave M8-61, M8-62 and Section 2 §10.1 unchanged — they are correct as written. Optionally add a parenthetical to M8-61 for reader clarity: "current status (Issued / Superseded / Withdrawn — the report lifecycle status, distinct from the series number states of M8-62)".
INDEPENDENT VERIFIER CONFIRMED THIS BECAUSE:
CORE CLAIM CONFIRMED, but the reviewer's reasoning is half wrong and the fix would make things worse.

WHAT IS REAL (independently verified):
The public-verification status banner is given three different vocabularies in three different parts, and the disagreement is specifically Cancelled vs Withdrawn.
- parts/section_1_foundation.md L98: "genuine, current, superseded or cancelled"; L180 (§4.1 Public Verifier): "the certificate's live status (valid, superseded, cancelled)"; L327 (§6.1 M9 row): "genuine, current, superseded or cancelled". The word "withdrawn" appears nowhere in Section 1 as a report status (only "withdraw consent" at L179), and the glossary defines "Superseded" (L296) but neither Withdrawn nor Cancelled.
- parts/section_3_functional_core.md L1392 (M9-14): "GENUINE — VALID, GENUINE — SUPERSEDED, GENUINE — WITHDRAWN, or NOT GENUINE" — no Cancelled.
- parts/section_5_nfr_plan.md L164 (NFR-69, [MUST], with a black-and-white print acceptance check) — a THIRD variant the reviewer did not find: "the words GENUINE / NOT GENUINE / SUPERSEDED / CANCELLED". A developer implementing the banner from NFR-69 ships CANCELLED; from M9-14 ships WITHDRAWN. That is a concrete, testable conflict on an enum, so Major is fair.

Part C and Section 2 are internally consistent and clearly authoritative: WF-18 and L633/L634 of section_2_workflow.md, M8-51/M8-55/M8-59, M9-14, and the M8 field table at section_3 L1317 ("Report state | Draft / Pending approval / Approved / Issued / Superseded / Withdrawn") all use Withdrawn. front_matter.md L19 also says "genuine, current, and not amended or withdrawn". Section 1 and NFR-69 are the outliers.

WHAT DOES NOT STAND:
1. The M8-61 vs M8-62 sub-claim is a misreading. "Issued / Cancelled / Missing" is the established NUMBERING-SERIES state vocabulary, used identically in WF-99, WF-100, WF-102 (section_2 L773-776), M1-71, M1-72 (section_3 L176-177) and M17-37 (section_4 L882). M8-62 says "cross-checks the numbering series and flags any number that is neither Issued nor Cancelled" — that is the state of a NUMBER in a gap-free series, orthogonal to M8-61's report-status column. Section 2 §10.1's "A cancelled report keeps its number" is the same numbering concept. The two are legitimately different axes; no contradiction.
2. "The developer cannot enumerate the status column" is overstated. "One of the five states" at section_3 L1464 has an explicit referent 50 lines above in the same module: M9-17 ("Four distinct failure states exist", listing four) plus M9-18 ("A fifth, distinct state covers the report-substitution attack"). Five is traceable. It is imprecise (five signature-check states vs four M9-14 banners) but not unenumerated, and it is not evidence of a Cancelled state.

WHY THE PROPOSED FIX IS WRONG:
Adding "GENUINE — CANCELLED" and promoting Cancelled to a report lifecycle status creates NEW contradictions with at least five places: the M8 report-state field table (section_3 L1317) which has no Cancelled; M8-51/M8-59, which define exactly three post-issue mechanisms (amendment, replacement, withdrawal) and no cancellation; M9-09, which grants a verification token only to "every issued report version", so a cancelled (never-issued) number has no QR and the CANCELLED banner would be unreachable dead code; the M9-14 disclosure table (L1403-1404) and the M9 field table (L1475-1476), whose conditional rows cover only superseded and withdrawn; and it makes "five states" collide with the different five of M9-17/M9-18. The fix also keeps "cancelled" on the public page in Section 1, perpetuating the original defect.

Severity is Minor, not Major, because (a) the substantive assessor need is already met — M20-37 gives a filterable audit-trail extract "for producing to an assessor", M21-13 prints the matrix, NFR-120 makes every register readable without the software, and M21-81's "escorts for external assessors" shows the document's operative model is escorted access; (b) M21-11 makes the matrix "data, not code", so an Auditor role is configurable at go-live without a code change, which defeats "the developer will not build it" for everything except the database-level property; (c) the HQ half is largely subsumed by M20-38/40/42/44 (return plus immutable, locked, read-only, printable snapshots), and cross-unit consolidated reporting is an explicit non-goal (N9) deferred by OPEN-Q-T28, so gap #48's "Important" rating is already dispositioned rather than ignored. Fix defects: (1) ID collision — M21-14 already exists ("Every permission denial shall be logged...") and M21-15 already exists (the blinding rule); M21 runs continuously to M21-82, so the proposed IDs would duplicate two live requirements and, if renumbered, break every cross-reference through M21-82.

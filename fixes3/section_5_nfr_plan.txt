FINDING 3 of 7  [Major]
LOCATOR: §25.2 Transaction tables — `txn_receipt_allocation`
EXACT TEXT IN THE DOCUMENT:
| `txn_receipt_allocation` | Which receipt settles which invoice or advance — supports part payments and running accounts. | `receipt_id`, `invoice_id`, `amount DECIMAL(14,2)` | `id` | both | — | `invoice_id` |
PROBLEM:
The table can only allocate a receipt to an invoice. M17-13b [MUST] requires the opposite: applying an advance to an order "shall be recorded as a row in the 'Applied to orders' child table of the originating advance Payment", and the M17.6 field table defines that child table as "order, amount applied". An advance is taken before any invoice exists — that is what makes it an advance — so with `invoice_id` as the only target there is nowhere to record the application, and M17-13b's acceptance check ("Post one advance of Rs 10,000 and consume it across three orders; the customer ledger is correct at each step, exactly one Payment record exists") cannot be built on this schema. The shape is a leftover of the withdrawn design, where the ERP held the running account and the LIMS only needed to know which invoice a receipt settled; §25.2 accordingly names no customer-ledger table at all, and M17-21, M17-46 and M20-30 ("the totals reconcile to the customer ledgers") have no storage behind them.
REQUIRED FIX (paste-ready):
Replace the row with:

| `txn_receipt_allocation` | Which receipt settles which invoice **or which order**, and what remains unapplied — supports part payments, advances taken before any invoice exists, and the running account. Exactly one of `invoice_id` and `order_id` is set: `invoice_id` where the receipt settles an issued document, `order_id` where an advance is applied to work not yet invoiced (M17-13b). Unallocated receipt amount is the customer's advance balance and is never a row here. | `receipt_id`, `invoice_id BIGINT NULL`, `order_id BIGINT NULL`, `amount DECIMAL(14,2)`, `allocated_at`, `allocated_by_user_id` | `id` | `receipt_id`, `invoice_id`, `order_id` | — | `invoice_id`; `order_id`; `receipt_id` |

and add, in §25.6 or §25.2's notes: "`CHECK ((invoice_id IS NULL) <> (order_id IS NULL))` — an allocation targets an invoice or an order, never both and never neither."

Extend DB-37 to read: "A receipt's allocations cannot exceed the receipt amount, and an invoice's paid amount cannot exceed its total. | `CHECK (paid_amount <= total_amount)` plus a trigger summing `txn_receipt_allocation` (over both invoice and order allocations) against `txn_receipt.amount`."

Add one sentence to the §25.2 preamble: "**The customer ledger of M17-21 is a derived view, not a table:** it is `txn_invoice` (charges, credit notes and debit notes) plus `txn_receipt` (money in and, under M17-33a, money out) plus `txn_receipt_allocation` (what has been applied to what), ordered by document date. There is deliberately no stored balance column, because a stored balance and its underlying documents cannot be made to disagree if the balance is never stored."

FINDING 6 of 7  [Minor]
LOCATOR: §25.1 preamble (line 438); columns at lines 446, 465, 468 and 511; M22-07 (section_4 line 1785)
EXACT TEXT IN THE DOCUMENT:
`is_provisional BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT false`, `erp_customer_id`, `is_active`
PROBLEM:
Four `erp_` columns survive in the schema — `mst_customer.erp_customer_id`, `mst_consumable.erp_item_id`, `mst_equipment.erp_asset_id`, `txn_invoice.erp_invoice_id` — with nothing anywhere in §25 saying they are dormant placeholders for a system that does not exist. Keeping them is the right call, but unlabelled they read as live foreign keys, and the first developer to see one will look for the process that populates it. They also disagree with M22-07, which names six identifiers: `erp_receipt_id` and `erp_employee_id` appear in the requirement but in no table. And no table carries the "last-synchronised timestamp" M22-07 also demands, which is correct today but leaves the requirement and the schema visibly out of step.
REQUIRED FIX (paste-ready):
Add as a third paragraph of the §25 preamble, after the sentence ending "...with `p` total digits and `s` after the point.":

**Four columns are dormant and deliberately kept.** `mst_customer.erp_customer_id`, `mst_consumable.erp_item_id`, `mst_equipment.erp_asset_id` and `txn_invoice.erp_invoice_id` exist so that an external accounting system, if one is ever mandated, can be linked without a schema change to four large tables. While M22 is switched off — which is the only state this system is built and validated in — they are nullable, carry no foreign key and no constraint, are written by nothing and read by nothing. They are the only four: the `erp_receipt_id`, the `erp_employee_id` and the last-synchronised timestamp that M22-07 also names are **not** created now, and arrive in the migration that commissions the interface, if one ever is.
